Effective: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the "GTA VI Clips" application ("the App") handles information. Contact: clipsgtavi54@gmail.com.

1. Data collected

The App does not collect, store or share personal data of any other person. The only account used with the App is the channel owner's own TikTok account (@clipsxfvejr), used for login (TikTok Login Kit) and for uploading the owner's own videos (TikTok Content Posting API).

2. How data is used

Account information obtained through TikTok login is used only to authenticate the owner and to publish the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok account. It is not used for advertising, analytics or any other purpose.

3. Data sharing

No personal data is sold, rented or shared with any third party, except as technically required to communicate with TikTok's APIs for login and upload.

4. Data retention

No user data is stored by the App beyond what is needed to complete a login or upload session.

5. Your rights

Because the App has no other users, there are no other user accounts or stored personal data to access or delete. Questions can be sent to clipsgtavi54@gmail.com.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; the effective date above will be revised accordingly.
